The surprisingly good idea

Why send a funny anonymous message to a stranger?

Sometimes the best message is the one that makes a stranger laugh. You don't need a profile, a follower count, or even a name. A funny anonymous message can travel from one human to another with no expectation of likes or replies. Making someone laugh is a simple way to create a tiny human connection. Imagine someone having an ordinary day, opening a random message, and suddenly reading:

“Stranger, I have no idea who you are, but I hope your next meal is ridiculously good.”

A tiny message worth discovering

It takes only a few seconds to write. But for the person reading it, that tiny moment might be exactly what they needed.

The fun part

Anonymous messages don't have to be deep.

A message doesn't have to be profound to be memorable. It could be a terrible joke, a ridiculous observation, an oddly specific compliment, or something completely random. Try something like: “If nobody has told you today: you're doing better than your Wi-Fi signal.” That's the fun of sending a message to someone you don't know. You have no idea who will eventually read it or what kind of day they're having.
