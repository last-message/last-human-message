NO PROFILE   •   NO FOLLOWERS   •   JUST HUMAN WORDS
The strange part

You might not know who wrote it. You might never meet them. And somehow, that is the point.

The internet usually wants context before it gives you a person. Their name. Their age. Their job. Their followers. Their opinions. Their perfectly edited photograph pretending Tuesday was amazing.

This is different.

A message from a stranger arrives without the usual noise. It could be thoughtful. Ridiculous. Hopeful. Weird. It could make you laugh at exactly the wrong moment. It could remind you of something you forgot. Or it could simply say the one thing another human needed to hear.
