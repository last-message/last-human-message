Questions

What is Last Human Message?

Last Human Message is an anonymous chain of messages passed from one stranger to another. Someone leaves a message, another human discovers it, and the chain continues.

What exactly am I paying $2 for?

The $2 unlocks access to a message left by another person and lets you participate in the chain by leaving your own message.

Is Last Human Message anonymous?

The experience is built around anonymous human messages rather than public profiles, followers, likes or popularity rankings.

Is there an algorithm?

No follower counts, popularity rankings or traditional social media feed. The concept is intentionally simple: one human message, then another.

Why does it cost $2?

The project uses a small intentional transaction instead of advertising or attention-driven feeds. The goal is to make opening a message feel like a deliberate human interaction.
