Questions
What is Last Message Human?

A simple human-to-human project. One person leaves a message and another person may discover it later.

Who is this for?

Anyone who has something small to say, even if they don't know who will hear it.

Why make something this simple?

Because the internet is enormous, but human connection doesn't have to be complicated.

Is there an algorithm?

No follower count. No popularity ranking. No algorithm deciding whose words matter.
